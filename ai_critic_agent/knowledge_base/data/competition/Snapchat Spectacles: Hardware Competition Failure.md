# Snapchat Spectacles: Hardware Competition Failure

## Core Failed Assumption
**"A strong social media brand can win in consumer hardware without deep differentiation"**

---

## Case Overview
- **Company**: Snap Inc.
- **Founded**: 2016
- **Status**: Repeated failed iterations
- **Category**: competition

---

## What They Believed (Hypothesis)
Snap believed brand loyalty would drive wearable adoption.

---

## What Actually Happened (Reality)
Consumers saw no compelling reason to switch from phones.

**Key Metrics:**
- **Inventory Write-down**: $40M+
- **Repeat Purchase**: <5%

---

## Lessons
> "Brand does not replace product necessity."
