# Mixer: Streaming Platform Competition Failure

## Core Failed Assumption
**"Paying top creators can overcome entrenched platform network effects"**

---

## Case Overview
- **Company**: Mixer (Microsoft)
- **Founded**: 2016
- **Shut Down**: 2020
- **Total Funding**: Microsoft internal
- **Category**: competition

---

## What They Believed (Hypothesis)
Mixer believed exclusive contracts with top streamers would pull audiences from Twitch and YouTube.

**Specific Claims:**
- Creators bring audiences
- Cash incentives beat ecosystem lock-in
- Microsoft brand adds leverage

---

## What Actually Happened (Reality)
Audiences followed temporarily, then returned.

**Key Metrics:**
- **Market Share**: <5%
- **Creator Retention**: <12 months
- **Shutdown**: Platform closed

---

## Why It Failed

### Primary Reason: Competition (Ecosystem Lock-in)
Audiences follow platforms, not paychecks.

---

## Lessons
> "Creators don’t migrate audiences permanently."
