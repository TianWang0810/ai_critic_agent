# Webvan: E-Commerce Timing Failure

## Core Failed Assumption
**"Consumer demand would arrive before logistics economics matured"**

---

## Case Overview
- **Company**: Webvan
- **Founded**: 1996
- **Shut Down**: 2001
- **Total Funding**: ~$800 million
- **Founders**: Louis Borders
- **Category**: timing

---

## What They Believed (Hypothesis)
Webvan believed online grocery demand was imminent and invested heavily in automated warehouses before consumer behavior and logistics costs were ready.

---

## What Actually Happened (Reality)
Demand existed but infrastructure costs made the model unsustainable.

**Key Metrics:**
- **Burn Rate**: $1B in 5 years
- **Order Density**: Far below breakeven
- **Unit Economics**: Permanently negative

---

## Lessons
> "Being early is indistinguishable from being wrong."
