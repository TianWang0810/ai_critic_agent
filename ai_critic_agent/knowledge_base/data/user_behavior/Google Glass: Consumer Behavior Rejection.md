# Google Glass: Consumer Behavior Rejection

## Core Failed Assumption
**"Consumers will tolerate always-on cameras in public spaces"**

---

## Case Overview
- **Company**: Google
- **Founded**: 2013
- **Status**: Consumer product abandoned
- **Category**: user_behavior

---

## What They Believed (Hypothesis)
Google believed technological novelty and hands-free utility would outweigh social discomfort.

---

## What Actually Happened (Reality)
Strong social backlash (“Glasshole”) killed adoption.

---

## Lessons
> "Social norms override technical novelty."
