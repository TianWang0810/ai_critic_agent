# FTX: Trust and Governance Failure

## Core Failed Assumption
**"Speed, intelligence, and ambition justify bypassing basic governance controls"**

---

## Case Overview
- **Company**: FTX
- **Founded**: 2019
- **Collapsed**: 2022
- **Total Funding**: $1.8B+
- **Founders**: Sam Bankman-Fried
- **Category**: general

---

## What They Believed (Hypothesis)
FTX leadership believed exceptional talent and speed mattered more than traditional controls, audits, and governance.

**Specific Claims:**
- Informal controls are sufficient
- Trust can replace oversight
- Speed beats compliance

---

## What Actually Happened (Reality)
Lack of governance enabled massive fraud and misuse of customer funds.

**Key Metrics:**
- **Customer Funds Lost**: $8B+
- **Audited Financials**: None
- **Collapse Time**: Days

---

## Why It Failed

### Primary Reason: Governance Neglect
Trust is infrastructure, not optional.

**Evidence:**
- No board oversight
- No segregation of funds
- No internal controls

---

## Lessons for Startup Critique

### For Similar Assumptions
> "Trust without controls scales risk."

**Red Flags:**
- Founder-controlled finances
- Lack of audits
- Informal operations at scale

### Counterargument Template
> "FTX demonstrates that speed and intelligence cannot compensate for absent governance in high-trust systems."
